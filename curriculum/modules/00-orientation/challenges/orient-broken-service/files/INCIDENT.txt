INCIDENT 0xBEAC — Silent Port
=============================
Beacon failed its health checks when the last lab image was built.

What students see:
  - "./service/beaconctl.sh start" shows errors or stops
  - curl to the health URL → connection refused

Do not rebuild anything. Fix the local config (and the data path). Start
the service on localhost. Then read the flag from the live /health answer.
