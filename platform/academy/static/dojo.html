Zoom in on one station.

It is a wave. The wave turns on and off.

Read the wave.

On means 1. Off means 0. These are bits.

Eight bits make a byte.

A byte is a number. 01010011 is 83.

Bytes become words.

83 is the letter S. The signal says SIG!
