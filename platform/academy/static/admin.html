Curriculum admin

Add challenges with a guided wizard, or save curriculum suggestions for later review.

Turn dojos and challenges on or off

Uncheck a dojo or a challenge to hide it from learners: it disappears from the dojo, the profile and ./academy, and cannot be started. Check it again to bring it back. Progress is kept either way.

Launch any challenge

Admin mode unlocks every belt. Pick a challenge to open it in the dojo, then press Start there.
